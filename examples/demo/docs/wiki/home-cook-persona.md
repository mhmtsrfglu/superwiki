# Home cook persona

**Who:** cooks at home three to five nights a week, mostly from a small rotation of recipes, and
tries something new about once a week.

**In the kitchen:** phone on the counter, hands wet or floury, patchy signal.

**What they want from Ladle**
1. Open a saved recipe instantly, even offline ([[offline-sync]]).
2. Follow it without touching the screen much (cook mode, [[M-03]]).
3. Send it to someone and have it look good in the chat ([[W-02]]).

**What they do not want:** an account with a password ([[magic-link-auth]]), a feed, or a life story
above the ingredients.
