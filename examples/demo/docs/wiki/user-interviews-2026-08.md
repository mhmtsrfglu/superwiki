# User interviews, August 2026

Eight 30-minute interviews with people who cook at home at least three nights a week.
Raw notes: [interviews-2026-08.md](../raw/interviews-2026-08.md).

## Findings
- **Screens lock mid-recipe** (P1, P8). Led to cook mode: [[M-03]].
- **No signal in the kitchen** (P4). Led to [[offline-sync]].
- **Passwords are a reason to leave** (P6). Led to [[magic-link-auth]].
- **Link previews decide whether a shared recipe gets opened** (P5). Led to [[W-02]].
- **Recipes arrive as URLs** (P2, P7). Led to [[W-03]].
- **Unit conversion goes wrong by hand** (P3). Led to [[B-05]].

The people behind these findings are summarised in [[home-cook-persona]].
